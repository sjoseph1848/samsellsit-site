The short version

Cash offers should land under the ask

Cash buyers will likely come in below the $135,000 ask. I expect $110,000 to $125,000. The $125,000 to $130,000 stretch is only if the house shows better than feared and the rehab is lighter.

$155,000 is the same-street finished sale

1504 Lakeview is what an already-updated smaller house on the same street actually sold for: $155,000, after 150 days, under a $210,000 original list. Same year, 1,015 sq ft, 2 bed / 1.5 bath. That is a ceiling for a similar-size finished house nearby, not an as-is number for 1300 Lakeview.

$182,000 is the nearby as-is foundation sale

903 Travis shows what as-is foundation product nearby cleared at: $182,000. It is larger (1,422 sq ft, 3 bed / 2 bath, 1995) and sold cash-only as-is with foundation issues, including a 3-inch foundation differential plus other systems. At $127.99 per square foot, 1,053 sq ft is about $135,000. This house is smaller and is 3 bed / 1.5 bath rather than 3/2, so the ask already sits at a discount to that sale. The expected offer is lower still.
